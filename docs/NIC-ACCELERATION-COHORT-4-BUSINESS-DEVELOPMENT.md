## 1. Problem–Solution Fit Canvas

### 1. Customer segment(s)

Pakistani e-commerce sellers operating on **Daraz**, **Shopify**, or both — typically SMB scale (**PKR 200K–5M+** monthly sales). Solo operators or small teams who handle listings, customer service, returns, and restocking themselves without a dedicated ops or data team.

### 2. Problems / pains + frequency

| # | Problem | Frequency |
|---|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **No unified view** of catalog, orders, returns, and performance across marketplaces | Daily — sellers check multiple dashboards |
| 2 | **COD returns and RTO** quietly erode margin; marketplace dashboards show revenue, not true profit after fees, shipping, and failed deliveries | Weekly/monthly reconciliation; pain spikes after campaign periods |
| 3 | **Reviews and return reasons** pile up as unstructured text; issues repeat because no one has time to read and act on them | Continuous — worsens as order volume grows |
| 4 | **Competitor blind spots** — sellers don't know how rival listings are priced, ranked, or keyword-optimized on Daraz/Shopify | Weekly when launching or repricing SKUs; daily during campaigns |

### 3. Triggers to act

- A bad month where returns spike and the seller cannot explain why  
- Preparing for a sale/campaign and unsure which SKUs are actually profitable  
- Opening a second sales channel (e.g. Shopify alongside Daraz) and drowning in duplicate manual work  
- A peer or community post about a tool that saved time on returns or reviews  
- Failed delivery costs showing up in the courier/marketplace statement  
- A competitor outranking them on the same SKU or winning with better listing copy/keywords  
- Hunting for a new product to launch and manually scrolling competitor stores for hours  

### 4. Emotions before / after

| Before | After (when problem is solved) |
|--------|--------------------------------|
| Anxious, reactive, guessing | In control — knows what to fix first |
| Distrust of headline revenue numbers | Confidence in **real** margin |
| Overwhelmed by reviews/returns | Relief — issues summarized with clear next steps |
| FOMO on competitors | Grounded view of what is working on each channel |

### 5. Available solutions (pluses & minuses)

| Alternative | Pluses | Minuses |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| Manual spreadsheets | Free, flexible | Breaks at scale; no live data; error-prone |
| Native Daraz / Shopify dashboards | Official, reliable per channel | Siloed; no cross-channel view; limited AI on reviews/returns |
| Zoho Inventory, Linnworks | Multi-channel sync | Not COD/Pakistan-aware; no sentiment or return root-cause AI |
| XenonHunt | Daraz/Shopify product research | Research-only; no live competitor tracking, ops agents, or profit/COD layer |
| Ginkgo Retail | Pakistan omnichannel OMS | Order/inventory focus, not AI agent layer for reviews/profit |
| Manual competitor stalking | Free | Time-consuming; no alerts, SEO/keyword suggestions, or side-by-side product comparison |

### 6. Customer limitations

- **Budget:** Low tolerance for enterprise pricing (PKR 15K+/month tools are a hard sell for SMB sellers)  
- **Time:** Cannot spend hours in dashboards — needs mobile-first, glanceable insights  
- **Digital literacy:** Varies; UX must be simple, Urdu/English friendly over time  
- **Trust:** Hesitant to connect store credentials until value is proven (freemium helps)  
- **Device:** Primarily smartphone; desktop-only tools under-serve this segment  

### 7. Behavior + intensity

| Behavior | Intensity |
|----------|-----------|
| Checks marketplace seller apps daily | High |
| Asks questions in Facebook/WhatsApp seller groups | Medium–high |
| Makes pricing/restock decisions on gut feel | High (especially without analytics) |
| Ignores or bulk-replies to reviews | Medium — until a bad trend hits |
| Copies listings manually across channels | Medium — painful for multi-channel sellers |
| Scrolls competitor stores and Facebook groups for product ideas | Medium–high — especially before launching new SKUs |
| Guesses at listing titles, tags, and keywords | High — most sellers lack SEO tooling |

### 8. Channels of behavior

**Online:** Facebook seller groups, WhatsApp business groups, YouTube seller tutorials, Daraz/Shopify seller communities, LinkedIn (agencies), NIC/incubator founder networks  

**Offline:** Local wholesale markets ( sourcing relationships ), courier hub conversations, meetups at co-working spaces  

### 9. Problem root / cause

1. **Fragmented data architecture** — each marketplace owns its slice; no native cross-channel ops layer for SMBs  
2. **COD-heavy market structure** — payment method drives higher refusal/return handling cost, invisible in revenue charts  
3. **Unstructured customer feedback** — reviews and return reasons are text, not KPIs; SMBs lack NLP/data capacity  
4. **Margin opacity** — commissions, shipping subsidies, return shipping, and campaign fees are spread across statements  
5. **No real-time competitive intelligence** — marketplace search and social scouting are manual; sellers miss pricing moves, keyword gaps, and trending products in their niche  

### 10. Our solution

**Tijarah AI** — a mobile-first AI operations layer that connects a seller's stores, unifies catalog management, and deploys specialized agents for **sentiment**, **returns/COD risk**, **profit**, **inventory**, **competitor analysis**, and **product hunting**.

**Live today (paid tiers / beta):** AI review analysis, action plans, per-product return analytics with AI, product-level AI chat (native).

**Free tier (no AI):** Daraz + Shopify connection, unified catalog sync, one-click cross-platform publish **without AI-generated copy**, fetched order/return/review data with **rule-based calculations** (rates, counts, stock, pricing stats), and non-AI catalog/stock queries.

**Paid tiers (AI):** Sentiment agents, AI action plans, AI product chat, AI listing generation, predictive COD risk, profit agent, inventory agent, competitor analysis, and product hunting with keyword/SEO recommendations.

**High-level concept:** *"An AI operations team for e-commerce sellers — know your real profit, beat your competitors, and find your next winning product."*

## 5. One-Minute Pitch

Every seller I know on Daraz — myself included, since 2023 — fights the same battle. You're checking multiple dashboards, guessing at reorder points, watching returns pile up without knowing why, and never quite sure if you're actually making money once fees, shipping, and cash-on-delivery losses are counted. For many Pakistani sellers, especially in fashion and general merchandise, COD-related returns and failed deliveries can consume a double-digit share of revenue — that's real money disappearing every month, quietly.

**Tijarah AI** connects a seller's stores — Daraz, Shopify, and more over time — into one platform, and puts specialized AI agents to work: one reads through reviews and explains what customers keep complaining about, one breaks down return reasons so you can fix the product or listing, one will track true profit after every hidden fee, one will forecast inventory before you stock out, and two more will **track your competitors in real time** — comparing listings, surfacing better keywords and SEO — and **hunt trending products** in your niche before everyone else lists them. It works whether you sell on one marketplace or several. And unlike tools built for Western Amazon-first sellers, this one is designed for how Pakistani sellers actually operate — COD-heavy, multi-channel, and without a data team.

I've lived this problem while running Daraz stores and building AI products professionally. Tijarah AI is the tool I wished I had. **Know your real profit, beat your competitors, find your next winning product.**

### 9.3 Market analysis

**Structural tailwind:** COD remains dominant (estimates range **55–94%** of e-commerce orders by source). Return/refusal handling is a recurring, quantifiable margin leak — especially for fashion and newer stores — with no Pakistan-first AI ops product addressing it end-to-end.
